# Linen opening photography art direction

The opening row alternates product and people: Kapioo's original Chinese-style meal photo, the supplied photograph of two people with meals, Kapioo's Thai-inspired meal photo, an illustrative diner photo, Kapioo's Korean-inspired meal photo, and an illustrative after-work photo. The left product card uses a near-9:16 portrait frame and a tuned cover crop to fill it without empty bands while keeping the meal tray prominent. The two generated images are **illustrative** and saved as responsive WebP files in `public/marketing/`; they are not menu photography or customer testimonials.

The row repeats seamlessly as a slow horizontal ribbon. Visitors can drag or swipe it, while a soft paper-toned blur blends both ends into the page. Automatic movement stops while someone is interacting with the row, when it leaves the viewport, and when reduced motion is requested.

The later “We’ll do the cooking” scene uses three frames supplied from Kapioo’s commercial as one short visual story: unbox, press play, enjoy. The superimposed video subtitles were removed from copies of those frames; the photographed meal, packaging, phone, and mascot remain the visual subjects. The section uses a taller central phone frame on desktop and a compact two-row composition on mobile.

The visual direction is candid food editorial: direct flash, a little camera tilt, natural expressions, one graphic shadow, and simple ivory or warm brown surroundings. The people-focused sequence includes the supplied friends photo, a Black diner and an Asian diner. Keep the black tray and the food recognizable. Use cream, oat, walnut and softened black across the series. Avoid decorative props, busy room backgrounds, excessive food shine, and generic staged smiles. Pinterest mood references: [friends eating lunch](https://au.pinterest.com/pin/getting-some-shots-for-soul-origin-just-some-friends-eating-their-salads-for-lunch-at-the-beach-beach-healthyfood-f--549650329505015773/) and [candid food gathering](https://au.pinterest.com/pin/friends-gathering-in-2025--214272894766450972/). No third-party images were copied into the site.
